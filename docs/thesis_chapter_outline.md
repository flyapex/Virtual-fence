# Thesis chapter outline

1. **Introduction and problem framing** — virtual fencing, early pulse exposure,
   candidate role for pre-training, research aims and scope.
2. **Literature review** — virtual-fence learning and welfare; associative and
   social learning; simulation/perceptual validity; evidence gaps. Report the
   documented search protocol and screening flow.
3. **Conceptual framework and simulation model** — causal assumptions,
   Rescorla-Wagner/social-facilitation model, calibration-only datasets,
   sensitivity analysis and limits of inference.
4. **Materials and methods** — setting, animals, collar, simulation modality,
   welfare safeguards, three-arm group randomisation, outcomes and analysis
   plan. Include ethics approval and preregistration identifiers.
5. **Results** — recruitment/flow, adherence, primary group-level transfer
   outcome, secondary outcomes, welfare/adverse events and robustness checks.
6. **Discussion** — interpretation, transfer/perceptual validity, social effects,
   welfare trade-offs, comparison with literature, limitations and next steps.
7. **Conclusions and recommendations** — bounded conclusions, implementation
   implications and research priorities.

Appendices: search strings/log and PRISMA flow, ethics materials, detailed
protocol, collar parameters where permitted, code/data dictionary, additional
models and reproducibility instructions.
