# Draft systematic literature-search protocol

## Objective

Identify empirical and methodological research on cattle virtual fencing,
audio/electric-cue learning, pre-exposure or simulated training, social learning
and transfer to a real virtual-fence context.

This is a **draft protocol**. Register or timestamp the final version before
screening begins. It is designed to be reported using PRISMA-S/PRISMA principles
where applicable; no claim is made here that a completed systematic review has
already been conducted.

## Sources and date limits

Search CAB Abstracts, Scopus, Web of Science Core Collection, PubMed, AGRIS and
Google Scholar (supplementary citation chasing only). Search reference lists and
forward citations of included studies. Use database inception through the actual
search date; record platform, coverage, exact export date and all limits.

## Core concepts and Boolean structure

1. Animal: `(cattle OR cow OR cows OR bovine OR "beef cattle" OR "dairy cattle")`
2. Fence: `("virtual fenc*" OR "virtual boundar*" OR geofenc* OR "GNSS collar" OR "GPS collar" OR "containment system")`
3. Learning/cues: `(learn* OR train* OR conditioning OR "associative learning" OR "audio cue*" OR acoustic OR "warning cue" OR "electric pulse" OR stimulus)`
4. Transfer/simulation: `(transfer OR generalisation OR generalization OR pretrain* OR pre-expos* OR "virtual environment" OR immersive OR projection OR "mock pen" OR simulation)`
5. Social/welfare modifiers: `("social facilitation" OR "social learning" OR welfare OR behaviour OR behavior OR escape* OR "response latency")`

Use both a broad fence-and-learning search and a focused transfer/simulation
search. Translate field tags to each platform rather than assuming syntax is
portable.

## Eligibility

Include studies of cattle exposed to virtual-fence, audio, electric, boundary or
closely analogous learning procedures that report a behavioural, containment,
pulse/audio or welfare-relevant outcome. Include controlled experiments,
observational studies, technical validations and systematic reviews for context.
Do not exclude by language at search stage; document translation decisions.

Exclude studies with no cattle-relevant result, no accessible methods/outcomes,
purely economic/specification reports, duplicate publications, or studies where
the reported evidence cannot inform cue learning, welfare, containment, social
effects or transfer. Keep non-cattle studies only in a separately labelled
mechanistic/background set; do not pool them with cattle evidence.

## Screening and extraction

Deduplicate in a documented reference manager. Two reviewers independently
screen titles/abstracts and full text; resolve disagreement by discussion or a
third reviewer. Pilot the form on a small set before full screening. Extract
species/breed, group and animal counts, prior exposure, device/cue settings,
boundary type, context, outcome definitions, pulse/audio counts, escape rules,
welfare measures, social/group design, effect estimate, variance, limitations,
funding and conflicts. Appraise bias with a tool appropriate to design and do
not treat poor reporting as zero effect.

## Synthesis

Use structured narrative synthesis first. Meta-analyse only genuinely comparable
outcomes with compatible unit-of-analysis and sufficient group-level data.
Separate real-fence transfer trials from pen/maze, simulation and pre-exposure
studies. Treat group clustering, social facilitation and missing device logs as
prespecified heterogeneity/risk-of-bias issues.
